| 2026-08-01 | [personas](./2026-08-01-personas/) | done | Five people who would use Nora, three residents and two family members, spanning early to late stage, three languages and both genders, with every resident utterance existing as a runnable scenario. **The first run failed 7 of 39 and all seven were real defects.** The worst: "where is my husband", the sentence PROJECT.md section 6 uses as its example of the hardest question in the product, recognised the intent at 1.00 and then said nothing, because subject recognition matched names and not relationships while its doc comment claimed otherwise.
